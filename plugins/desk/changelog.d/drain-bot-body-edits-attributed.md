The issue-drain poller now decides whether a body change is a bot's from the issue's edit history instead of from who created the issue. A body that only bots edited since the last poll, such as a health table refreshed every few minutes, wakes nobody, and a person's edit still wakes the drain even on an issue a bot created or when a bot edited after it. Anything the history cannot prove (an unknown or deleted edit, an incomplete history, a snapshot that changed during the read, a changed title, a revision recorded before titles were kept) stays news. A failed history read is shown as the poll's error and moves no marker, cursor or ETag, and a failed poll no longer keeps the new list ETag that would have hidden its change. The GraphQL quota is kept apart from the REST quota. See [issue drains](docs/issue-drains.md).

Someone else assigning or unassigning an issue now wakes the drain (`assigned <login>`, `unassigned <login>`), while the drain's own assignment during its turn stays quiet. The edit history is read to the end however long it is.
