The issue-drain poller now decides whether a body change is a bot's from the issue's edit history, on issues a person created. A body that only bots edited since the last poll, such as a health table refreshed every few minutes, wakes nobody, and a person's edit among a bot's still wakes the drain. Anything the history cannot prove (an unknown or deleted edit, a history that cycles, a changed title, a revision recorded before titles were kept) stays news. The history is read page by page with progress kept in the poll state, so a very long one is finished across GraphQL quota windows, scans share the pages fairly, and an issue that needs no read is never held behind one. A read that fails or runs out of quota leaves only that issue for the next poll and is shown as the poll's error without waking the drain. The GraphQL quota is kept apart from the REST quota. See [issue drains](docs/issue-drains.md).

Someone else assigning or unassigning an issue now wakes the drain (`assigned <login>`, `unassigned <login>`), while the drain's own assignment during its turn stays quiet. A poll lock is no longer taken from a live owner however long it holds it, and is released only by its owner.
