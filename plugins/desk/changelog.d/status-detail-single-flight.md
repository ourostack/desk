`desk_status` no longer loses its index and readiness detail on a busy machine. It gives the runtime status a fixed budget of about a tenth of a second, and a computation that ran over was thrown away, so every call on a slow machine could answer "unavailable" while starting yet another computation. Now at most one [runtime status](mcp/src/runtime/desk-session.js) runs at a time, a call that finds one already running answers at once with the last detail, and a detail that arrives late is kept: the next call serves it, marked cached, with its start time in the new `status_detail_from` field. The lexical readiness smoke test, which failed on busy CI runners when a restarted server's first `desk_status` came back without its `lexical` section, now waits for a detail computed after the event it checks.
