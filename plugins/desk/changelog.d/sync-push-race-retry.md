The background push worker no longer gives up when another clone pushes between its rebase and its push ([ourostack/desk#234](https://github.com/ourostack/desk/issues/234)). It used to retry a rejected push exactly once with `git pull --rebase --autostash`; when the remote moved again in that window, the second push was rejected too and the worker recorded `push_rejected_after_rebase` and filed a Desk problem, although nothing was wrong. Now, while a push is rejected as non-fast-forward after a clean rebase, the worker waits a jittered, doubling backoff and runs another rebase and push, for at most four rounds ([`mcp/src/runtime/sync-worker.js`](mcp/src/runtime/sync-worker.js)). Any other rejection still stops after one retry. A remote that keeps moving past the last round still ends `push_rejected_after_rebase`, with the local commits intact, the desk not mid-rebase and nothing forced. The push runs with `LC_ALL=C` so its rejection text reads the same in every locale.
