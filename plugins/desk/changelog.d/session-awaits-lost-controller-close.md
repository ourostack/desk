A session now waits for a lost readiness controller to finish closing before it elects a new one, and before it reports itself closed ([`mcp/src/runtime/desk-session.js`](mcp/src/runtime/desk-session.js)). When every status check of a controller failed, the session closed it in the background and re-elected at once: the new controller child failed with `EADDRINUSE` on the endpoint the closing one still held, the session could join the controller that was exiting, and a session closed meanwhile could leave that child running. The wait is bounded, because a controller child that does not exit is killed after 1 s.
