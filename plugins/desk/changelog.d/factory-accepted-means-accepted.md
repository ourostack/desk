The Desk factory now counts an acceptance as accepted whenever the task's sign-off state is `accepted`, whether or not the record carries `verified`. A sign-off recorded through `task_signoff` is the agent recording the operator's word, and there is no separate human-verified state. Attention per accepted outcome, first-pass yield and the accepted count in the sign-off rollup all read `state` alone, so old records (with `verified` true or false) and new ones (with no `verified` field) count the same way, and attention per accepted outcome no longer reads "no data" once `verified` is gone. The first-pass yield of an accepted job is measured, the `signoff_unverified` reason and rollup count are gone, `accepted_unverified` stays in the sign-off rollup at 0 for older readers, and the reports say "recorded by the agent on the operator's word" where they said "verified". Refusals keep their `verified` flag for now.
