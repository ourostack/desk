Hosted Desk is safer at shutdown and after a stuck session. A single-desk gateway now gives Desk the gateway user's own home, so Desk reaches ready again and task and track handles stay the same between sessions; before, it ran with an unwritable home and degraded. Shutdown waits at most 12 seconds for Desk sessions to close, so one stuck session can no longer stop every other desk from pushing. When a desk's shutdown push fails, the gateway pushes its unsaved commits to a new `desk-rescue/<time>` branch on the person's own desk repository, which a newer commit can't reject, and logs whether that worked; the logs no longer claim a failed desk is kept on disk, because the container's disk goes with the replica. A desk whose work outlived its session no longer locks its person out until the replica restarts: their next session gets a plain "try again in a few minutes" while the work still runs, and once it has stopped, that desk's commits are pushed, its realm is emptied and a fresh one starts. Container Apps now gives shutdown 120 seconds instead of 90.
