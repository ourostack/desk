A session whose desk routes it away from a factory store can no longer be published to the store it left once its local records are lost. Before, `status.json` was the only durable record of "away" for a session that was never retracted, so after its marker was pruned and `status.json` was lost, corrupt or restored from an older copy, the flush published it to the old store: a held (quarantined) session released its newer copy there, and a copy derived before the desk moved and never delivered went out there. Now "away" lives where the copies live. Every flush moves the local copies of each session it does not place here (away, stale, stalled or unknown, held or not, delivered or not) out of the outbox into `retracted-copies/`, and every sweep does the same in each store's outbox, including a store whose consent is off, routing each session by its marker, else the desk folder its receipt recorded, else the desk its Claude Code transcript names; a session with no route at all is kept in every store. A desk folder that was recorded and no longer resolves (moved or renamed) is not here either. A kept copy goes back and publishes only on a positive route here, and without one it is frozen, never published and never deleted. A held session (facts quarantined) with no positive route and no derivation receipt is frozen the same way, and an older Desk's `private_plugins_missing` refusal is lifted only for a session that is here. A route back still restores and publishes, and when a newer derive is already in the outbox the kept copy is retired beside itself instead of lingering. Kept copies with no route back are counted in the local status (`kept_frozen`, with the oldest one's age) and in a `desk_doctor` line, so the contributions this costs are visible. See [factory-local-capture.md](docs/factory-local-capture.md).

Two signals that only `factory.js status` showed are now findings, printed as lines in `desk_doctor` and in the plain-text boot (starting `Factory: `, before the consent line), together with the orphan pass's finding, which the boot text did not print before: `retention` when the sweep's tombstone pruning fails (`prune_failed`) or the last orphan pass counted a delivered-copy prune that threw (`copies_prune_failed`), and `capture_check_unavailable` for a store this machine still contributes to whose own capture check could not read the record three or more times in a row.
