A session whose desk routes it away from a factory store can no longer be published to the store it left once its local records are lost. Before, `status.json` was the only durable record of "away" for a session that was never retracted, so after its marker was pruned and `status.json` was lost, corrupt or restored from an older copy, the flush published it to the old store: a held (quarantined) session released its newer copy there, and a copy derived before the desk moved and never delivered went out there. Now "away" lives where the copies live. Every flush moves the local copies of each session it does not place here (away, stale, stalled or unknown, held or not, delivered or not) out of the outbox into `retracted-copies/`, and every sweep does the same in each store's outbox for a session whose marker routes it to another store, including a store whose consent is off. A kept copy goes back and publishes only on a positive route here, and without one it is frozen, never published and never deleted. A held session with no positive route and no derivation receipt is frozen the same way, and an older Desk's `private_plugins_missing` refusal is lifted only for a session that is here. A route back still restores and publishes, and when a newer derive is already in the outbox the kept copy is retired beside itself instead of lingering. See [factory-local-capture.md](docs/factory-local-capture.md).

Two signals that only `factory.js status` showed are now pushed as findings in `desk_doctor` and the boot status, the way the orphan pass's finding is: `retention` when the sweep's tombstone pruning fails (`prune_failed`) or the last orphan pass counted a delivered-copy prune that threw (`copies_prune_failed`), and `capture_check_unavailable` for a store whose own capture check could not read the record three or more times in a row.
