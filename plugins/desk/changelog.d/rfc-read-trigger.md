The [`using-desk`](skills/using-desk/SKILL.md) foundation now says when to open the Agentic Engineering V2 RFC: before writing, reviewing or explaining anything about Desk or its method, such as docs, sites, pitches or onboarding. An agent had written a public site about Desk's method without opening it, because "on demand" named no moment. The RFC still does not load at every startup. To keep the startup context within its 9,700-character budget, the foundation no longer repeats the rule for raising older unsigned deliveries, which [`interaction-style`](skills/interaction-style/SKILL.md) already owns.
