The [`using-desk`](skills/using-desk/SKILL.md) foundation now says when to open the Agentic Engineering V2 RFC: before writing about or explaining Desk or its method for people, such as docs, sites, pitches or onboarding, or reviewing such writing. Ordinary code review of the Desk repository does not trigger it. An agent had written a public site about Desk's method without opening it, because "on demand" named no moment. The RFC still does not load at every startup. To keep the startup context within its 9,700-character budget, the foundation no longer repeats the rule for raising older unsigned deliveries, which [`interaction-style`](skills/interaction-style/SKILL.md) already owns.
