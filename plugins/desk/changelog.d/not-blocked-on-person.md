Interaction style now has an end-of-turn check: "needs your approval to merge", "requires your explicit instruction to commit and push", "only you can grant access and restart", "please click" and "waiting for you" are not the person's gates. Agents do that work or find the route, and end a turn on the person only for a product decision, a message sent as them, money, or their own identity proof.
