Desk Browser `mine` no longer refuses a read or a click on a live page such as Teams web when the page changes between its two native reads and the change event arrives a moment after the snapshot. One DOM round trip now delivers pending change events before the reader decides, so a changed page is read again instead of returning `AUTH_CONTROL_COVERAGE_REFUSED`; a mismatch with no change behind it is still refused. See the [owned reader](mcp/web-auth.cjs).
