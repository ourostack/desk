Multi-user Hosted Desk now has capacity limits. Each account can have 3 Desk sessions open, and the gateway 12 in all. A person's 4th session is refused with a message naming the limit, unless one of their own sessions has been quiet for 10 minutes and was most likely left behind, in which case that one gives way. At the total limit, a new session first takes the place of the person's own session quiet for 10 minutes, then another account's quiet for as long, so no session in use is ever closed for someone else's; a full gateway says so plainly. A new desk clone is refused with a clear message while the realm disk is over 80% full. At shutdown every desk pushes its unpushed commits at the same time, each as its own user, and all of them within 80 seconds of the stop signal so they finish inside the replica's 90-second grace period; a desk still being cloned no longer holds the others back. A desk whose push fails or runs out of time is logged by account and its realm is kept on disk. The gateway also publishes the `ouro` CLI's admin sign-in settings at `/.well-known/ouro-desk-admin` (tenant, CLI client id and the gateway's `desk.admin` scope), from `DESK_ADMIN_CLIENT_ID` and `DESK_ADMIN_SCOPE`, which provisioning sets from the identity record once the admin API is recorded.
