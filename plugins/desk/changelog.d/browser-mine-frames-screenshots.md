Desk Browser `mine` reads child frames and takes screenshots, and busy pages no longer refuse reads or refs ([#679](https://github.com/ourostack/desk/issues/679)). Each read is now one DOMSnapshot capture. It no longer needs the page to stay still across several reads, which a live page (a Dev Box settings page after `tab-select`, Teams, Stream) never did, so `AUTH_SNAPSHOT_INVALIDATED` now means only that the document was replaced during the read. A ref re-resolves to its own unchanged node when it is used, and a node the capture and the DOM read disagree on loses only its own ref. Same-process and out-of-process child frames (Stream's player and transcript, Teams' panes) show their content inside their `frame` node, and refs act in them through their owner or the frame's own session; the browser confirms each owner on the way still names that frame. Everything typed anywhere in the tab is scrubbed from every frame. A frame that can't be read at that moment shows empty and is counted in `framesOmitted`. `screenshot [<ref>] [--full]` saves a PNG of the tab or one element in the context's files folder, with every input, textarea and editable region masked in every frame, as a snapshot redacts them; open-shadow content is masked by its host, a region the mask missed while the picture was taken means it is taken again, and a page with a closed shadow is refused. `type` focuses its target and lets it settle before sending keys, so a remote desktop no longer loses the first characters. `tab-select` brings its tab to the front, and `tab-close` takes an optional index. Saved-login stores stay excluded, password and typed values stay redacted, and the private profile is still deleted on close. See the [owned reader](mcp/web-auth.cjs).
