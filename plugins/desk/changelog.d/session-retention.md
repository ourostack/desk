Session retention keeps the disk that agent sessions use bounded. Old recordings, traces, screenshots, browser profiles, build folders, clones and worktrees used to pile up with nothing to remove them. The new opt-in [session-retention CLI](mcp/scripts/session-retention.js) removes what inactive sessions no longer need, keeps their history and plan, and never removes a checkout whose work is not on a remote. It reads the Copilot app's database read only and leaves sessions alone when it cannot. A ceiling option prunes the oldest inactive sessions until storage is under a limit. It is a dry run unless given `--apply`, and it deletes slowly enough not to load a busy machine. [Session retention](docs/session-retention.md) lists what it removes and keeps, its options, and daily launchd and systemd schedules.
