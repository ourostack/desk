The factory's improvement loop now runs by itself. A problem the factory finds becomes an improvement card on your desk (a file under `_meta/improvement/`), and a detached worker keeps the loop going from the card to a checked fix, with nobody asking for a pass. System friction recorded with `friction_add` opens its card at once, with no curator signoff.

At session start, a line says how many cards are open and how old the oldest is, and the agent hands the oldest to a background subagent with the new `improvement_next` tool (`improvement_open` and `improvement_update` file and finish cards). Cards are standing, pre-authorized work: the agent decides, fixes, merges where the repository allows it and records every ruling in the pull request. You are asked only for money on your payment method, a credential or account only you can act in, or an irreversible destructive action.

The plugin now starts the waste evaluator itself, only on a subscription sign-in. Under per-token billing or an unknown sign-in it does not run, spends nothing and says so at session start. `DESK_FACTORY_LOOP` set to anything other than `1`, `true`, `on` or `yes` turns the whole loop off, and `DESK_FACTORY_HEADLESS_EVALUATOR=0` turns the evaluator off alone. The factory documentation has a new "The improvement loop" section.

The loop's health record states every number the way the rest of the factory does: `{ state, value, reasons }`, with `value: null` and the reason named when a number could not be read, never a bare 0.
