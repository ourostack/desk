The factory build now writes the data the store's Lean walk needs, without changing any existing figure. Each `jobs/<job>.json` timeline adds the job's workers, human turns and pull requests on the job clock (a pull request's time only when the facts carry it, so a public desk's stay withheld), the lead window, the job's work bursts (runs of agent work split by 15 idle minutes or an operator turn) and the gaps between them with what each waited on. Bursts and gaps add up to the lead time. Each placed session also gets a swimlane file, `jobs/<job>/<session id>.json`, with the job's lane intervals and the evaluator's stretches, whose evidence points into that interval list. Three new rollups sit next to the existing ones: `rollups/stackup.json` splits each job's lead time into segments that add up to it exactly, `rollups/tasks.json` gives each job's compact answer (lead time, working time and the value-adding part of it, flow efficiency, waiting by cause, top three causes, longest gap, burst count), and `rollups/causes.json` ranks the causes of waste by time across finished, fully labeled jobs. Every figure in them carries its number state.

Each `waiting` stretch now has a `waited_on`: `next_prompt` (from the agent stopping to the next prompt, nights included), `api_retry`, `tool_failure`, `long_tool_call` (a successful call of five minutes or more) or `unknown`. A stretch where another worker of the job was working is not waiting: the new figures split that part off as unlabeled agent work. The muda measures and the waste Pareto keep their meaning; each Pareto adds `waiting_corrected_ms` and `agents_working_unlabeled_ms`, which add up to its `waiting` row. On the store's data when this was built, the correction moves 4.25 of 84.68 labeled waiting hours (15,288,160 of 304,851,291 ms, 5.0%) to agent work. The split looks at one session at a time: a worker of the same job in a concurrent session does not yet split a wait. Labels from a session several jobs share, where the evaluator labeled the whole session or two jobs carry the same labels, make the new figures partial with `labels_from_shared_session`.

The [factory evaluator](skills/factory-evaluator/SKILL.md) now says that a stretch where any worker of the job is doing work is not `waiting`, and that the work should be labeled instead. Because that changes what a `waiting` label means, the rubric moves to version 3: new labels carry `"rubric":"3"`, and labels written under rubric 2 are still read and published as before. `rollups/stackup.json` carries `basis: "wall_clock_in_lead_window"`, because its waste totals count wall-clock time inside the lead window once and so do not match the muda rollup's sums.
