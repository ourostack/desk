A freshly created desk's `.gitignore` now also ignores `.machine-local.yml` and Office lock files (`~$*`). The lock files appear whenever a Word, Excel or PowerPoint document inside the desk is open, and showed up as untracked changes; `.machine-local.yml` was already documented as gitignored in [directory structure](skills/directory-structure/SKILL.md) but [first-run bootstrap](skills/first-run-bootstrap/SKILL.md) never wrote it. Existing desks keep their own `.gitignore`; add the two lines by hand if you want them.
