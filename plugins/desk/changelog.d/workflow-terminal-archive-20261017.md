Terminal task updates now archive their complete task directory as part of the update rather than relying on a later remembered move. The returned path and commit proof describe the final placement. Existing evidence, lifecycle history, unsigned delivery and resource-disposition rules remain; archival does not accept work, archive a track or remove resources.

Canonical writers and normal sync share an ephemeral owner claim. Automatic archival preflights placement and companion changes, commits pinned admitted bytes once, and reports partial or uncertain outcomes without rolling back external edits. Archived same-terminal status-only retries are read-only; mixed archived updates refuse without replay. See [terminal lifecycle and recovery](skills/task-lifecycle/SKILL.md) and the [MCP API contract](mcp/README.md).

Destination ancestry is revalidated around relocation, and HEAD/index destination occupation refuses before the card write even after an external deletion. Admitted Git bytes account for inherited destination text normalization while task-local attributes refuse before writing. Live push contention receives bounded retries and retains its obligation in the existing sync status. Crash-owner recovery remains fail-closed and unqualified: owner-generation inspection alone cannot prove orphaned Git/hook closure.
