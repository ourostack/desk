**A Desk move, rename, or archive no longer rewrites a task card's YAML just to make its one real edit.** `task_move`, `track_rename`, and `task_archive`'s terminal-status bump used to read a card into an object, merge in the new field, and dump the whole object back to YAML — changing quote style, adding `.000` milliseconds to timestamps, folding a long single-line scalar into a `>-` block, and reordering or dropping comments on every card the tidy touched, even though only `track:`, `title:`, `updated:`, `merged_into:`, `status:`, or `factory_report:` actually changed. They now call a new [`patchMarkdownFrontmatter`](mcp/src/util/fm.js), which finds each named top-level frontmatter line, replaces just that line and that field's own continuation lines with the new value, and appends a field that's genuinely new — leaving every other byte of the card, including quoting, date formats, block scalars, key order, and comments, exactly as written.

[`isCardHousekeeping`](mcp/src/factory/desk-repo.js) also recognizes more of what a real tidy commit produces in history already committed, and in cards other tools still touch directly. A frontmatter scalar now counts as unchanged when it's semantically equal after parsing: a date-only value (`2026-05-26`) equals its midnight-UTC timestamp form, with or without `.000` milliseconds, and a folded (`>-`) or literal (`|-`) block scalar equals the single-line scalar with the same text. A card's body counts as unchanged if it only gained or lost exactly one trailing newline. Anything the parser isn't confident about — a field nested inside a list item, a block scalar with blank lines or a `+` chomp, a changed key order — still counts as real work, matching every other case `isCardHousekeeping` already treated conservatively.

Deriving a commit's diff-tree entries and rename substitutions now happens once per commit `sha` instead of once per card touched, cached for the life of one desk-reader instance, so a tidy commit touching many cards no longer re-derives the same substitutions for each one.

Verified against two real tidy commits: a 60-card personal desk (10 of its cards touched; 9 now correctly excluded as housekeeping, up from 1 before this change) and a 40-card work desk (22 cards touched; 19 now correctly excluded, up from 16). The cards still counted as real work in each case are real work for reasons specific to each: a genuinely new card, a field nested inside a list item that this judge doesn't parse, and a card that gained an actual new field alongside a real body edit.
