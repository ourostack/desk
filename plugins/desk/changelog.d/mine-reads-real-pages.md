The `mine` browser reads ordinary signed-in pages instead of refusing nearly all of them (#679). On Desk 3.2.76 every snapshot of a real page failed, even `example.com`: a user-agent root on anything but a form control (an SVG `<use>`, `<details>`, a video's controls), a `<template>`, two layout rows for one node, a custom element's unslotted text, or a page still loading each refused the whole output, and binding a ref per element (1,391 on GitHub's home page) ran past the command timeout. Now browser-internal user-agent trees and inert template content are recorded but never walked or rendered; a node's repeated layout rows must agree; a shadow host's unslotted light children may be missing from the capture (shadow content and controls still may not); refs bind when an action uses them, after the document is re-qualified as unchanged, and the element handle is disposed after the action; a snapshot invalidated by a live page is retaken up to five times; and whitespace and checkbox or radio markup values no longer redact every matching word. Frames, closed shadow roots and open-shadow actions still refuse, so Teams and other framed apps remain out of scope (see the [browser roadmap](../../docs/roadmap/browser.md)).
