New [`fast-honest-ci`](skills/fast-honest-ci/SKILL.md) skill for keeping CI as fast and cheap as it can be without hiding a failure, for every desk. It covers measuring the critical path and the queue before changing anything; fixing slow tests, time-balanced shards with a guard, lockfile caches, fail-closed path selection and pull-request-only fail-fast and cancellation; standard runners first, paid or larger runners only with an approved budget, per-repository budgets, scarce runner pools and yielding to the default branch; merge-queue build concurrency sized to the runner pool; a wall-clock budget guard that reports and ratchets; and cause-first flake handling, with one recorded re-run only for a filed failure signature. GitHub's limits and prices are cited from its documentation, and [`examples.md`](skills/fast-honest-ci/examples.md) works through a public component library and two private repositories.
