A detached derivation that fails for a reason other than an unreadable or missing log now leaves a diagnostic in the local `status.json` under `derive_failures`, keyed by the session's marker name: the time, the step that failed (`route`, `source`, `derive`, `bind`, `write_facts`, `write_jobs`, `write_receipt`, `read_marker`, `wait_quiet` or `lock`), the error code and a short message with quoted text and paths removed. Before, a refused write (a Windows rename over an open file, for example) was swallowed and nothing showed that a session's facts were not derived. The result of `deriveMarker` and `deriveFile` is unchanged and the marker is kept, so a later flush still retries; the next successful derivation clears the record. A missing or non-regular log is expected and records nothing. Only the newest twenty records are kept, and nothing is published.
