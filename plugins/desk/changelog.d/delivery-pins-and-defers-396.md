Private delivery now leaves the shared checkout's branch, index and files untouched, including identical untracked paths. Source following is explicitly deferred. Delivery pins its destination and credential-route configuration, verifies the pinned remote after a push, and does not apply an old target's acknowledgement to a changed origin. Ambiguous routes and unscoped or full delivery evidence are reported as unverified. Executable pre-push hooks, including LFS hooks, remain explicitly blocked until native remote-name arguments and tracking isolation are qualified; required policy is never bypassed. Private replay and the existing Desk conflict policy remain unchanged; this is not complete source recovery or LFS-readiness proof.

Connection resets and other ambiguous push failures retain an unconfirmed candidate even with a non-null exit code: the remote may already have accepted it. Only confirmed all-ref rejection is retried automatically; authentication denial remains distinct, and unknown outcomes never claim accepted commits exist only locally.
