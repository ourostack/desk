`track_rename` and `task_move` now check the cards they will update before they move anything ([`mcp/src/tools/move.js`](mcp/src/tools/move.js)). `track_rename` patched each task card after the folder had moved, and `task_move` edited the tasks tables in `track.md` after the task folder had moved, so a card with invalid YAML, such as a duplicated key, stopped either tool half way. Both now refuse with nothing changed, and the error names the card and the YAML problem.
