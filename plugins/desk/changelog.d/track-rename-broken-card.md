`track_rename` now checks every task card in the track before it moves the folder ([`mcp/src/tools/move.js`](mcp/src/tools/move.js)). A card whose frontmatter cannot be updated, such as one with a duplicated key, used to stop the rename after the folder had moved and before the cards were updated. The rename is now refused with nothing changed, and the error names the card and the YAML problem.
