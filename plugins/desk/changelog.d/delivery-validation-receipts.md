A delivery that asks the operator "accept or send back?" now leads with a validation receipt. The receipt names what the operator is accepting in one plain sentence, lists each acceptance check with its result and proof (a screenshot or rendered capture of the live result where it is visible, a primary-source link otherwise), says what to look at and says what is not covered. A substantial delivery publishes it as a page and links it. The operator had been asked to accept work with nothing to show what they were accepting.

[`evidence-discipline`](skills/evidence-discipline/SKILL.md) owns the rule in a new "Validation receipt at delivery" section, building on "Visual proof when it helps". The [`using-desk`](skills/using-desk/SKILL.md) foundation, [`task-lifecycle`](skills/task-lifecycle/SKILL.md), [`interaction-style`](skills/interaction-style/SKILL.md), [`session-resumption`](skills/session-resumption/SKILL.md), the session-start [details](skills/session-start/details.md), the `task_update` and `task_archive` sign-off note, the boot instruction for unsigned deliveries and the `task_update` and `task_signoff` tool descriptions point at it instead of the old three bare lines.
