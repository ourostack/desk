Hosted Desk gains a browser session for its own pages and the admin routes `ouro desk` calls. A page sign-in through Ouro sign-in (`/signin`) starts a 12-hour `__Host-desk-session` cookie (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax) that ends at once when the account's Desk access is turned off or the account is deleted; page changes need the session's CSRF token from the gateway's own origin. `POST /admin/invites`, `POST /admin/accounts/:id/access` and `DELETE /admin/accounts/:id` need an Ouro access token carrying the `ouro.admin` app role: an invite for a new person creates their account, and deleting an account removes its identities, invites and binding. A sign-in finished in another browser than the one that started it still gets the "start again" page and leaves the invite unused.
