Friction about the shared system now becomes a kaizen card. `friction_add` takes `about`: `setup` (the default) stays on the desk as before, and `system` files an issue labeled `kaizen` in the desk's factory store with the store's recorded consent and account, a generic `title`, and a generated card block carrying the `signal` and `evidence_jobs` when known ([kaizen-file.js](mcp/src/factory/kaizen-file.js)). A filed card leaves only its URL on the desk; a card that cannot be filed, or whose text holds anything credential-shaped, a home or drive path, or an email address, stays on the desk with the reason. The `friction-management` skill says which friction is about the system and how to word a public card, `lesson-capture` opens cards from the evaluator's waste, and `curator` becomes the kaizen worker: it files system friction, ships each card's countermeasure, fills in `version` when the release lands, closes confirmed cards and reverts or re-plans not-confirmed ones.
