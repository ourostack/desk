The `curator` skill's kaizen-worker steps now say plainly that a card's `countermeasure` and `version` may never be set while it is still a draft (no `signal` or `hypothesis`): a fix shipped for a card no one ever named the measure for cannot be judged or completed later, because the free text that would explain it stays on the filing desk, not on the public card. A card already stranded that way has a recovery path: reconstruct `signal` and `hypothesis` from the desk's own friction record when that's possible, otherwise leave the card open with a comment saying why, and close it under the next rule once its `before` side runs out. That next rule names a closing path for a card whose `before` side, given how the fleet updates, will not gain another job — once every session in the store already runs the plugin at or after the card's `version`, the comparison stays at `0` jobs on that side build after build — close it on its merged, verified countermeasure (merged, with tests, on green CI) instead of waiting on a verdict this comparison will not produce, and say so in the closing comment; andon keeps watching every tracked plugin's releases for a quality regression independent of any kaizen card's state, so this closing path does not turn off that alarm.
