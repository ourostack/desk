The `curator` skill's kaizen-worker steps now say plainly that a card's `countermeasure` and `version` may never be set while it is still a draft (no `signal` or `hypothesis`): a fix shipped for a card no one ever named the measure for cannot be judged or completed later, because the free text that would explain it stays on the filing desk, not on the public card. The steps also add a named closing path for a card whose `before` side can never gain another job — once every session in the store already runs the plugin at or after the card's `version`, the comparison stays at `0` jobs on that side forever — close it on the merged, verified countermeasure instead of waiting on a verdict that cannot arrive, and say so in the closing comment.
