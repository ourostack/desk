Interaction style now asks for money once. When a plan needs anything physical or paid, agents sweep the whole plan at plan time (each device's ports, drivers and firmware, network and power where it runs, cables and spares) and present every item as one list with exact listings, prices and delivery checked, as one decision with a recommendation. A need found after a purchase is a planning miss recorded as friction, not a second piecemeal order. See [interaction style](skills/interaction-style/SKILL.md).
