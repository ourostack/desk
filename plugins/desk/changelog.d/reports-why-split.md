Factory reports now split the time spent waiting for the operator's next prompt by why the agent stopped ([`walk.js`](mcp/src/factory/pipeline/walk.js)). Each entry of `timeline.waits[]` gains `worker`, `why`, `why_source` (`rule`, `evaluator` or `none`) and `confidence`. A rule classifies a mechanical stop at high confidence: a token cap, a rate limit, an API error or a refusal is `error_limit`, an interrupt is `interrupted`, an open question tool is `question` and a plan tool is `approval`. Every other wait takes the evaluator's stop label (labels `/3`) whose wait is exactly the human wait it is part of, else its why is `not_known` with one reason in `reasons`: `not_labeled`, `could_not_tell` (the evaluator's `unknown`), `outside_own_share` (the facts do not say which part of the session was the task's) or `not_in_published_facts` (older facts carry no stop). Every worker's `human_wait` is now listed, so a subagent's recorded ask wait is classified like any other. The task's next-prompt waiting is split by why on each gap and burst (`idle_by_why_ms`), on each `rollups/tasks.json` row (`next_prompt_by_why_ms` and `not_known_by_reason_ms`) and `rollups/stackup.json` row (`next_prompt_by_why_ms`), and the longest gap names its `why`. The split adds up to `waiting_by_waited_on_ms.next_prompt` exactly, and every part carries that figure's state, reasons and bound; while some of the time is not known, each class is a lower bound (`stop_partly_classified`). Next-prompt time that no recorded wait holds, which the reports computed but did not publish, is now `not_known_by_reason_ms.stop_not_recorded`. In `rollups/causes.json`, the `waiting:next_prompt` row gains `children[]`, sub-cause rows `waiting:next_prompt:<why>` with `parent: "waiting:next_prompt"` that add up to it; the ranking and its total are unchanged. Stops never enter class or waste totals. A stop label that no longer matches its facts, because the facts were derived again after it was made, is left out on its own: that wait reads `not_labeled` until the evaluator labels it again, and the file's stretches still count when they match.
