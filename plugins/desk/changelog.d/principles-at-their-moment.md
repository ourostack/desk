Five working principles now sit in the skills that run when they apply, instead of in one operator's notes:

- [`evidence-discipline`](skills/evidence-discipline/SKILL.md) "Prior art: steal, don't copy": find and cite existing work, build only the gap, and take the reason prior art works rather than its surface, naming what you took and why. Its "Visual proof" section adds: look at what you show before you show it.
- [`interaction-style`](skills/interaction-style/SKILL.md) "Respond before editing": a question is not a request to act.
- [`work-orchestration`](skills/work-orchestration/SKILL.md): defer work that waits on approval from people outside the operator's authority, and a lead or coordinator session delegates rather than investigating or fixing inline.

More rules from the same notes:

- [`work-orchestration`](skills/work-orchestration/SKILL.md): think a plan through with an independent reviewer before asking for go; every loop that queues or merges pull requests has a named owner; merge only on the final head's own green checks; and never hold a merge for a nit, which becomes a fast-follow.
- [`git-hygiene`](skills/git-hygiene/SKILL.md): spend disk like money (reuse clones, partial and sparse clones, remove worktrees and stop preview servers when done); never `git stash` in a shared checkout; never put a credential where it can be seen.
- [`evidence-discipline`](skills/evidence-discipline/SKILL.md): test from the state a real person starts in, not a fresh install, and isolate test copies of an app by construction rather than environment variables.
