Every pull request now passes the Tim check before it reaches GitHub or a reviewer, and the rules it checks are Desk's own. They adapt [Tim Clem's Copilot instructions](https://github.com/tclem/dotfiles/tree/main/copilot), without his AI signature:

- [`pr-surface-hygiene`](skills/pr-surface-hygiene/SKILL.md) PSH-012: the body says why the diff exists in a short paragraph; it never recaps the diff, narrates how the work went or reports CI, and nuance goes in code or review comments. PSH-008 now asks for every template section filled, with before and after captures for a change a person sees. PSH-013 is the Tim check: before opening a PR, changing its body, marking it ready or requesting review, the agent itself asks whether Tim Clem would approve its shape, against PSH-012, the three rules below and green checks on a head that includes the current base.
- [`evidence-discipline`](skills/evidence-discipline/SKILL.md) "A test earns its place": a new or changed test names the regression it catches and fails with the fix reverted; "no new test" is acceptable.
- [`runtime-symptom-investigation`](skills/runtime-symptom-investigation/SKILL.md) "Fix the one place it broke": no backstops stacked on a real fix.
- [`pr-feedback-on-own-pr`](skills/pr-feedback-on-own-pr/SKILL.md) "Each review item is a hypothesis": accept or dismiss only with evidence, check every member of a set a claim covers, reply to every thread and resolve only what the evidence settles.
