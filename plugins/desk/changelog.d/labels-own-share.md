A job's waste now counts only its own part of each session. The evaluator labels a whole session, and a session can hold several jobs, so the factory's rollups counted a shared session's waste once per job: on the store, five finished jobs shared one session of about 114 hours, and a job with a 0.8 hour lead time carried 114 hours of labels. The build now cuts each job's labels to its binding's segments before any figure uses them, so `muda_time`, the waste Pareto, rework's defect time and the job pages count each job's share, and the shares of one session add up to it once. Labels whose job's share the facts do not record (a subagent-only binding, or one of several bindings from before segments were published) are left unused with the new reason `share_unknown`, so the job reads as not fully labeled instead of carrying the whole session. The evaluator brief now carries `own_share`, the job's spans of the session, and the [factory evaluator skill](skills/factory-evaluator/SKILL.md) labels only those spans.
