A job's waste now counts only its own part of each session. The evaluator labels a whole session, and a session can hold several jobs, so the factory's rollups counted a shared session's waste once per job: on the store, five finished jobs shared one session of about 114 hours, and a job with a 0.8 hour lead time carried 114 hours of labels. The build now cuts each job's labels to its binding's segments before any figure uses them, so `muda_time`, rework's defect time and the job pages count each job's own part. A segment marked `shared` is held by several jobs and each of their `muda_time` figures counts it, while the waste Pareto counts each session's time once. Labels the build cannot place on the job are left unused, so the job reads as not fully labeled instead of carrying the whole session or a zero: `share_unknown` when the facts do not record the job's part (a subagent-only binding, one of several bindings from before segments were published, or any binding from a public desk, which publishes no segments), and `outside_share` when the labels have no stretch inside it. The evaluator brief now carries `own_share`, the job's spans of the session, and the [factory evaluator skill](skills/factory-evaluator/SKILL.md) labels only those spans, and writes no stretches when the share is unknown.
