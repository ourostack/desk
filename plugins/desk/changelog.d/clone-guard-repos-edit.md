`task_update` now refuses a `repos` change that drops or renames a repo of a card whose next step or blocker says the work is on another machine, unless the same call also rewrites `next_step` so it no longer says so. Before, an agent whose clone was denied by the elsewhere-clone guard could remove or rename the repo on the card and clone anyway (found in boot acceptance round AL). The refusal names the one way through: record the operator's word with `next_step`. Edits that keep every repo name, and cards with no such marking, are unchanged.
