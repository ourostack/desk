`desk_doctor`'s `switch_state_branch` repair now says why when admission after the switch does not reach ready ([`mcp/src/runtime/desk-session.js`](mcp/src/runtime/desk-session.js)). It used to report only the degraded state, so a `degraded:admission_exception` after a successful switch did not name the error. The answer adds the admission `summary` and the error's name, message and code, with credentials and absolute machine paths redacted, because it is often pasted into a public report; `desk_status {detail:true}` keeps the full diagnostic.
