Each event a drain poll records now carries `why`, the one change that made the issue news (`new issue`, `title or body edited`, `comment <id> by <login>`, a label change or `reopened`), so an unexpected wake can be traced to its signal. Tests pin that a cross-reference from a pull request, the drain's own branch or not, never wakes a drain and that a reopen after the PR's close still does. See [issue drains](docs/issue-drains.md).
