A write or read no longer comes back refused as `admitting` from a session that is already admitted. A background admission attempt, such as the one a `.git/HEAD` change or a retry on the backoff starts, could begin after a tool call passed its own admission check and before the call's turn to dispatch; the call then refused with "Desk is still admitting" although the session reported `ready`. The call now joins that attempt and checks admission again, at most three times and within its usual wait, and still never dispatches while an attempt is running ([`src/runtime/desk-session.js`](mcp/src/runtime/desk-session.js)).
