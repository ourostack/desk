Desk's direct-call spend ledger is safer under concurrency and failure: a token whose account lookup got no answer now holds its reads (`spend_held`) instead of bypassing the quota floor, a token GitHub names no account for is floored on its own answers' numbers and counted by `unattributedSends()`, every quota observation is appended to a per-window log and Desk's reads are decided from the lowest remaining in it rather than from the `<kind>.rate` cache the shim reads (which is written once per observation and can briefly be higher), reads are held with the warning `DESK_SPEND_OBSERVATIONS_UNREADABLE` when those logs cannot be read, and parallel first calls with one token send a single `GET /user`. See [relays](docs/relays.md#quota).
