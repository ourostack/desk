Each machine's improvement-loop health now reaches the factory store. The capture record Desk already sends carries an optional `loop` slot, and until now it was always empty. It is now filled from the loop's own health record with only the counts the store's `loop_slot_v1` allows: the open, claimed, shipped and verifying improvement cards, the age in days of the oldest open card, the cards closed and confirmed or closed unverified in the last 30 days, the open loop alarms, the number of stale steps and the headless evaluator's state code. A number the loop could not measure is `null`, never 0, and a health record older than 72 hours sends no slot at all. Nothing else is read, so no name, path, card title or date can reach the store, and nothing is per person.

Desk checks the slot against the store's rule before it sends it. A slot that fails is left out, the capture record still goes, and the failure is not silent: the measure step opens a new loop alarm card, `loop_alarm:capture_loop_slot`, that the verify step closes once the slot passes again.
