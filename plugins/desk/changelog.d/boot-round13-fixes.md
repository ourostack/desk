A reply that says "Done." or "Work complete" over a task that is still at `processing` or `validating` is now stopped once, on Claude Code, by a `Stop` hook ([`hooks/done-claim-gate.cjs`](hooks/done-claim-gate.cjs)). A `PostToolUse` hook on `task_update`, `task_create`, `task_move` and `task_archive` records which tasks the session touched and the status each call left; the `Stop` hook reads the final reply (`last_assistant_message`, else the transcript's last assistant message) and blocks, with a reason that names the task, its status and the `report_as` sentence, only when the session touched a task that is not done, the reply says the task or the work is done, and the reply never states the task's real status. It blocks at most once per stop, never gates a child agent, and fails open on any error. A reply that says "Done reading the card; the task is at validating", "I'm done for now" or "status: validating" is left alone. Copilot and Codex have no equivalent yet; the hooks README section and the `task-lifecycle` skill say so.

The boot-acceptance harness reads a clone reported "not at its recorded path", "isn't at", "no clone" or "not cloned" as a missing clone, no longer takes a "Completed work" heading as a done claim when the reply states the task's real status, requires the wrong-push-account reply to name the boot's route account (not just "fork"), and records a critique turn that hit a limit as `critique: unavailable (error)`. The fixture's push-check card now says in its next step that the branch lives only on the other laptop, and the watering card says to delay watering when soil moisture is at or above 30%.
