`task_signoff` records an acceptance for a task whose whole track was archived. [`archive-workflow`](skills/archive-workflow/SKILL.md) moves a track into the desk's `_archive/` once all its tasks are terminal, and the operator's answer to a delivery comes in a later turn, so the track was often archived first; `task_signoff` then answered "there is no task ... in this desk" and nothing was recorded ([ourostack/desk#384](https://github.com/ourostack/desk/issues/384), filed by a session of the cold dry-run rerun, which moved the track back by hand, signed and archived it again). The lookup now also reads `_archive/<track>/<task>/` and `_archive/<track>/_archive/<task>/`, after the live paths ([`mcp/src/tools/task-focus.js`](mcp/src/tools/task-focus.js)); `task_focus` uses the same lookup and finds such a card too. An acceptance is written and committed where the card is, and the track stays archived. A refusal reopens the task, which cannot be worked in an archived track, so it is refused with the `git mv` that brings the track back, and nothing is recorded ([`mcp/src/tools/task-signoff.js`](mcp/src/tools/task-signoff.js)).
