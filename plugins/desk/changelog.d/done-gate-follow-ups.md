Follow-ups to the fail-closed done-claim gate. The gate now reads a card's `status:` with the same reader as its sign-off (`outcome.js`), so a quoted status, a status with a trailing comment or one in another form is judged, and a card whose status the reader cannot parse counts as unreadable for an acceptance claim. When a card is unreadable the block now says Desk could not check the acceptance, not that it is unverified, because a verified acceptance may be recorded on a card that moved. A transcript over 8 MB is read from its last 2 MB instead of letting the reply through; a reply the host does not send at all still passes.

The gate's quiet failures are now visible without blocking anything. Its own Stop error, a failure to record a touched task and a failure of the hook wrapper (a bad payload or a module that will not load) are counted in a small local record, and `desk_doctor` shows a "Done-claim gate" warning with the count and the time of the last failure while that failure is under a week old.
