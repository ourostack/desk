# Work that waits on outside approval

Defer it. Don't start, propose or ask about work that needs approval from people outside the operator's own authority (another team's sign-off, an identity or app registration, a policy exception) unless the operator asks for it. A review the repository requires before merging is not outside approval: request it as `work-orchestration` "Merging and the terminal state" says. Record the options you researched where the work's owner will find them (an issue or the task card), mark the work deferred and carry on with what needs nobody else. The operator's own decisions are not outside approval.

Deferring is not stopping. Pick up the next item that needs nobody else in the same turn. Don't ask the operator whether to contact the other party, and don't draft the request for later: either is starting the work. If the operator does ask for it, sending anything to another person in their name still goes through `operator-voice-comments`.
